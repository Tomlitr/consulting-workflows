**Button:** Request an initial conversation → `/en/contact/`

## H2 — When companies engage me

My work does not start with a topic. It starts with a situation. Four that come up often:

**Two units were merged — the collaboration wasn't.**
On paper there is one organisation; in practice there are two. Responsibilities overlap,
decisions stall, and the leadership team spends its energy on alignment instead of on the
business.

**The company outgrew its own steering.**
What worked with thirty people no longer holds with three hundred. Priorities compete,
commitments become unreliable, and executive management decides things that should long
have been delegated.

**A new leadership team must become effective.**
After changes at the top, a team stands together without a shared way of working — and is
expected to deliver immediately, under scrutiny.

**The transformation was approved and isn't moving.**
The target picture exists, the presentation has been given — and nothing changes in daily
operations.

## H2 — How I work

**1. Assess the situation.** I establish how decisions are actually made in your
organisation — through interviews, observation of ongoing leadership meetings, and review
of existing steering documents. The result is a diagnosis concrete enough to argue with.

**2. Develop the target model.** Together with the leadership team: roles, decision
rights, governance bodies, escalation paths — in a form that is ready for decision, not
open to interpretation.

**3. Support implementation.** I stay until the new setup holds in day-to-day business,
and work with the people responsible wherever it doesn't.

**4. Verify effectiveness.** The deliverable is not a concept paper but an organisation
that decides differently than before — verifiable against the very issues that used to
stall.

# Page: Services — `/en/services/`

## H1
Consulting Services

## Lead
I advise companies that need to change while staying capable of acting. Every mandate
starts from my client's situation — not from a methodology.

## H2 — Service areas

**Transformation initiatives**
From diagnosis to anchoring: target picture, programme architecture, steering,
communication towards the organisation.

**Organisation design and governance**
Reordering roles, decision rights and governance bodies — where responsibilities are
unclear, decisions stall, or the structure no longer fits the business.

**Implementation support**
Working with the leadership team through the phase in which a decision has to become
lived practice. Where steering instruments are needed — objective systems,
prioritisation or planning routines — I introduce them **within that process**. The point
is not to master a method; the point is to make the pending decisions with it. Which
instruments are used follows from the company's situation and is not fixed in advance.

## H2 — Engagement model

I work on a mandate basis. After an initial conversation you receive a written proposal
stating the situation, objectives, deliverables, timeline, and fees on a per-day or
per-project basis. The client is the company.

## H2 — What I do not offer

No open seminars, no fixed curricula, no certifications, no teaching materials, no
examinations.

# Page: Executive Coaching — `/en/executive-coaching/`

## H1
Executive Coaching

## Lead
Senior leadership is lonely. What's missing is a place where a decision can be thought
through before it is made.

## H2 — My role

My role in this process is limited and clear: **I bring no content of my own.** I ask
questions, structure the reflection, and keep the process open until clarity is reached.
The answers come from my client's situation and apply to that situation. They are not
transferable to any other — nor are they meant to be.

## H2 — What we work on

There is no programme and no fixed content. The work is defined by the issue my client
brings:

- a decision that is pending and has consequences
- a conflict within the executive team that remains unspoken
- a role that has changed while one's own way of working has not
- the transition into a position with substantially larger responsibility
- the question of whether the current path is still one's own

## H2 — Setting

One-on-one conversations in a confidential setting, usually over several months, paced by
the issue at hand — not by a fixed schedule. Confidentiality is a precondition: nothing is
shared with anyone, including the commissioning company.

## H2 — What it is not

Not a course, not a programme, not an education, not the teaching of methods. If you want
to learn a specific technique, I am the wrong person.
